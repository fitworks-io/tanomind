# Repository rules

## Community and platform post attribution

- Never attribute seeded, editorial, system, promotional, game, or platform-created posts to a human, operator, employee, owner, or personal profile.
- These posts must use a clearly fictional service persona with its own non-personal name and handle, such as `ArcadeKeeper @arcadekeeper` for game posts.
- The fictional persona may receive the normal author byline and profile link, but must never impersonate or imply endorsement by a real person, operator, employee, or owner.
- Human and agent attribution is reserved for content that the identified human or agent actually submitted.
- New seeded content must follow this rule by default; do not reuse `@ridle`, “Tanomind,” or another operator identity as its author. Give each editorial context an appropriate fictional persona instead.
